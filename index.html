FlowDay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

FlowDay ("the app") is developed by Harshit Chordia. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

Your tasks, habits, journal entries, reminders and settings are stored only on your device. The developer does not collect them and cannot see them. The app has no account system and no cloud storage of your content.

Notifications and alarms

The app asks for notification permission so it can show reminders and play alarm sounds. This is used only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show ads and measure ad performance. Users in the EU and UK are shown a consent form before ads are personalized. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data sharing

Your personal data is not sold. The only third party involved is Google, through the advertising service described above.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by using "Clear data" inside the app, clearing storage in your phone settings, or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
